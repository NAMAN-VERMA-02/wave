Wave

Press the button once to let Wave use your camera. Then try each gesture below; the box shows what the camera sees. The preview closes when you leave this page, and nothing is recorded.

✋ Open palmPlay / pause
👍 Thumbs upVolume up (hold to repeat)
👎 Thumbs downVolume down (hold to repeat)
✌️ Peace signForward 10 s
☝️ Pointing upBack 10 s
✊ FistMute / unmute

Hold a gesture for half a second. Alt+W turns Wave on or off.
